Custodian Guards:

With the new Custodes models coming to the Horus Heresy later this year, I decided to work on a small force of Custodes, starting with some Sentinel Guards.

Building:

There were no major conversions made to this unit during building. I chose to have the unit comprise of 4 guards, 3 of them with the sentinel warblades and the final one with a magisterium vexilla. The last model in the kit was made into a shield captain with paragon spear.
