Alpha Legion Breachers:

The first hobby project I have begun to undertake this year is finishing painting my Zone Mortalis List for Alpha Legion, starting this with the new plastic breacher squads.

The only picture I took of the unit during assembly was the sergeant, pirmarily because of the "rope" on his chest. To create this I got some thin wire, put it in my pin vice, and spun it around itself. This made a very nice looking rope effect
